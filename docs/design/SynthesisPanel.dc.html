You · 07:11 How does this relate to on-demand buses?
Meridian
Two mechanisms connect Silver Zones to on-demand bus provision, and they pull in opposite directions.
Lower zone speeds raise door-to-door time for any vehicle routed through them, which the operational literature treats as a scheduling cost. Against that, the same treatments raise the share of elderly residents willing to walk to a pickup point, which one press item reports as reducing the stops a route has to serve†.
No source in the graph measures both effects together. That gap is the interesting part.
