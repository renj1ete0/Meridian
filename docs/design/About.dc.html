Meridian
A line to measure everything else against.
Meridian crawls public sources on walkability, on-demand buses and autonomous-vehicle deployment, and relates what it finds into one graph. Every claim keeps the chunk it came from. Where two sources disagree, both are kept and the pair is marked — the system does not resolve the conflict on your behalf.
